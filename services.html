What We Treat

Our Services

AlignPro clear aligners are orthodontist-designed to correct a wide variety of dental spacing and bite conditions safely, comfortably, and with clinical precision.

Crowded & Gapped Teeth

We resolve overlapping, crowded teeth by gently expanding or shifting placement, and close open spacing gaps to prevent food traps and restore balance.

Overbites & Underbites

We correct vertical alignments where the upper front teeth overlap the lower teeth excessively, or when lower teeth protrude past upper front teeth.

Crossbites & Open Bites

We address complex bite misalignments where teeth don't fit together normally when closing, improving overall chew force distribution and speech.
